## Description (paste into the Store listing)

ByteProof is an AI proofreading assistant for academics, students, and writers. It works directly in Microsoft Word with tracked changes and reviewer comments, and polishes your writing in any other app — email, browsers, editors, and chat windows.

How it works:

• In Microsoft Word, ByteProof reads your document and returns professional revisions as tracked changes, with context-aware comments for citations, maths, and technical language.
• Anywhere else, select text and press Ctrl+Shift+' to replace it with polished writing that fits the surrounding context.
• Citation and maths protection keeps references and equations untouched.
• Choose from thesis, journal, general, and creative writing styles, with options for technical and academic tone.

Private by design:

• Local AI runs offline on your own computer — no account and no cloud upload for the default mode.
• Bring your own key: connect DeepSeek, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, xAI, Perplexity, or your own Ollama server for maximum quality.
• Global hotkeys work everywhere in Windows 10 and 11.

Start with a 7-day free trial of every feature. After the trial, continue with the limited free mode or unlock a one-time licence for up to two computers.

Note: the licence is activated inside the app after download; the Microsoft Store listing is free.
